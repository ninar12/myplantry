## Email 1 — The fridge photo (lead with this one, it answers the #1 complaint)

**Subject:** You don't need groceries to use MyPlantry — just a camera

The most common thing I hear is "I don't have any groceries to add." Here's the thing — you don't need a shopping trip or a receipt. Open MyPlantry, tap scan, and take a photo of your fridge or pantry shelf exactly as it is right now.
